What can I do with the demo?

Make yourself at home. Drag cards between columns, add tasks, click any card to edit its details, or switch to list view. You can also create a fresh board with the demo’s “New board” button.

Will my work be saved?

Your demo board is saved locally in this browser, so it’ll be here when you come back. It isn’t synced to an account or shared with other people. Clearing browser data removes your saved board.

Can I use it with my team right now?

This is a hands-on preview of Projile. The people shown on the board are sample team members. You can create an account and sign in. Shared workspaces and invitations are still on the way.
